# sketch_prime_number

This sketch visualizes prime numbers using an animated Ulam spiral.

## What It Shows

Numbers are placed on a square spiral starting from the center. Prime numbers are highlighted, while composite numbers are drawn as faint marks. As the animation fills in the grid, patterns begin to appear along diagonal lines and other structures.

## Why It Is Interesting

Prime numbers often feel random when listed in order, but this visualization shows that they still create visible geometric structure when mapped into space.

This makes the sketch useful for:

1. YouTube videos about math and patterns
2. Classroom demonstrations of prime numbers
3. Creative coding projects in Processing
4. Exploring how simple rules can create surprising visuals

## Controls

1. `SPACE` pauses or resumes the animation
2. `R` restarts the sketch
3. `UP` and `DOWN` change the drawing speed
4. `+` and `-` change the cell size and rebuild the spiral

## Good Video Angles

1. Why do prime numbers form diagonal patterns?
2. Are prime numbers random, or do they hide visual structure?
3. What happens when you map math into space?
